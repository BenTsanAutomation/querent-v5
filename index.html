Frequently asked.

01How is pricing structured?−
We quote fixed‑price per build, scoped to an outcome metric. No hourly billing. Most builds land between $8k–$45k; retainers and strategy hours start at $4.5k/mo.
02Do you replace our staff?+
No. We replace the worst parts of their week. Every deployment includes an approvals layer so humans stay in the loop on judgement calls.
03What stacks do you work with?+
n8n, Voiceflow, Vapi, Zapier, Make, OpenAI, Anthropic, HubSpot, Salesforce, Google Workspace, Slack, Airtable, Notion, Twilio. If it has an API, we can wire it.
04How long until we see ROI?+
Workflow automations typically pay back within 30–60 days. Concierge agents within 90. We model this in the strategy phase before you commit.
05Who owns the system?+
You do. Every build is delivered with full source, credentials, and SOPs in your accounts. We can stay on retainer or hand off entirely.
